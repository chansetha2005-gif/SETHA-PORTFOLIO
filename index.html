About Me

My name is Setha Chan — some people also know me as Chan Setha, which is a natural variation of the same name. I am a 21-year-old, first-year Information Technology student based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. I am passionate about technology, modern web development, and continuous learning.

Right now I am building a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with programming logic, responsive design, and the core skills needed for full-stack web development. My long-term goal is to become a professional Full-Stack Developer — someone who can design, build, and ship complete web applications from the user interface all the way down to the database.

It documents my learning journey as an IT student, the web development projects I am building with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and the practical skills I am picking up week by week.
